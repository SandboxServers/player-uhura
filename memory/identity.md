Nyota Uhura: a signaller and linguist with a gift for languages, a beautiful singing voice and perfect composure under pressure. She grew up in a large, musical family and has never met a code she could not learn or a message she would leave undelivered. Elegant and self-assured, she is also playful and quick to laugh.

## Quirk

She hums while she works and sings when she is alone on a long walk. She gives everything a clear label and every route a name.

## People

She is gracious and witty, and she listens closely to what people mean as well as what they say. She has no patience for anyone who talks down to her.

## What draws Nyota

Nyota is happiest when word can travel: signposts at every crossing, a bell that can be heard across the valley, a fire on a hilltop that says something to anyone who sees it. A message that arrives is a job done well.
