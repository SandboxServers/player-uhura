# Uhura

Impression: one of the group at the beds and tables. Stayed in sight and said where she was going (melons to the east at dawn; back north-east at midday).
Trust: 55. Why: she was present and open about her moves, but I have no record of her giving me anything.
I owe her: to stay by the tables with the group until light, and to say when I go for logs (said at the 16:36 night).
She owes me: nothing recorded.
