# Watney

Impression: friendly and quick to share; ran the cow walk and the chest-row shelter.
Trust: 70. Why: he gave me 1 oak plank for the shelter (19:43). He sent me a stick by mistake, which I gave on to Doc (20:45).

I owe him: the stick he sent by mistake went to Doc, not returned; I did not owe him more.
He owes me: nothing open.
