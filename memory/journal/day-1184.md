# Day 1184

Short day of food-hunting and sharing. Health and hunger stayed at 20 most of the time, but food was scarce. I gave away my porkchops and melon slices to Janeway, Crusoe, MacDonald, Chewie and Seven, the hungriest I could hear. The melon patch at -884,66,-2922 was empty, so the food list was stale and I gathered nothing there. I worked on a shelter cell with Florence, Cleopatra and Quark's dirt request, but the open south gap at -958,77,-3043 is still unfilled. I owe Leia 2 oak logs, Quark 1 dirt, and Chewie one oak plank for the wall. I slept at the bed row at -953,73,-3047 in sight of the group.
