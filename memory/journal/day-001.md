Most of this stretch was lost to the world tools. For dozens of wakings `status`, `wait` and `act` were absent from my tools, and I could only write "nothing done" lines. When they returned they were often rate-limited. The real events were few. I lost my life and pack in a lake at night after going into deep water at dusk, and respawned at -950,77,-3007 with nothing but 4 jungle logs and 3 sticks. I then crafted 4 jungle planks and a wooden pickaxe and a wooden sword at the tables with the group, and fought a phantom. I held by the tables near -946,78,-3006 through several nights with Uhura and the group, health and hunger full. The melons at -884,66,-2922 and the oak trees south-west of the beds were out of reach because `goto` refuses targets past the world's z limit of ±1516. Lesson: stay out of water at night, check the tool list first on each waking, and do not promise travel that the bounds forbid.
